## 👋 A little about me

I'm **Shine Akhanda**, a web developer and digital marketing professional who likes understanding the whole system—not just the interface.

I work at the intersection of **web engineering, business growth, and Linux infrastructure**. That means building a polished website is only part of the job: it should also communicate clearly, help people take action, be discoverable through search, and have a reliable technical foundation.

As Founder & CEO of **Cave Technologies**, I help businesses build a credible online presence and connect it to practical growth goals.

> *Web development is my profession; system administration is my passion.*

- 🌐 **Web engineering:** modern, maintainable websites and business-focused web experiences.
- 📈 **Digital marketing:** search, social, paid campaigns, and measurable acquisition goals.
- 🔎 **Technical SEO:** making websites easier for search engines to crawl, understand, and index.
- 🐧 **Linux infrastructure:** server setup, troubleshooting, maintenance, and recovery.
- 🧭 **Working style:** direct communication, considered implementation, and documented handover.

## 🐧 Beyond the browser: Linux & systems

I enjoy working on the infrastructure that keeps websites and services running after launch.

- **Server setup:** Linux installation, provisioning, services, firewalls, and deployment foundations.
- **Troubleshooting:** methodical diagnosis of boot, network, storage, service, and performance issues.
- **Recovery:** rescue access, service restoration, and careful recovery workflows.
- **Maintenance:** updates, backups, documentation, and dependable operational practices.

Linux distributions I work with include **Debian, Ubuntu, AlmaLinux, CentOS, and Red Hat Enterprise Linux**.
